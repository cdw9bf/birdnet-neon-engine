Legacy model weights files (.bn24; formerly also .bnv3) used with this engine
=============================================================================
Both formats are re-layouts (no retraining, no re-fitting) of models by the K. Lisa Yang Center for Conservation
Bioacoustics, Cornell Lab of Ornithology, and Chemnitz University of Technology (Kahl et al. 2021, BirdNET), and keep
the license of the model they come from:

- .bn24 files: BirdNET v2.4 (BirdNET_GLOBAL_6K_V2.4; the published BirdNET_v2.4_fp32.onnx).
  Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ , as the BirdNET-Analyzer README states for the models
  (https://github.com/birdnet-team/BirdNET-Analyzer; the Zenodo record https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15050749 lists
  CC BY-NC 4.0). The license text is also embedded in the header of every .bn24 file written by
  scripts/export_weights.py.
- .bnv3 files (BirdNET v3.0 preview; this library no longer reads them since its hand-written v3 engine was retired).
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ , plus the BirdNET+ V3.0 Developer Preview Terms of Use that come
  with the v3.0 model (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20703646, TERMS_OF_USE.txt).
  ShareAlike means these files may not be relabeled NonCommercial.

Both require the BirdNET attribution above.

does not cover these weights files.
