Older-hardware study of BirdNET v3.0 (2026-11-08): could v3.0 run usably on a Raspberry Pi 4 before the engine had a
path for it? Experiments H001-H005; this directory holds their summaries (the raw per-run data are not published).
H002: Cortex-A72 kernel calibration for a v3.0 path without dotprod or fp16 arithmetic, by bench/bench_a72_conv.c
(section 4 at 4 threads); it led to the neon-int8 / neon-fp32 packages.
